## The Challenge: The Failure of the Traditional Standup

The **Daily Scrum** was originally devised as a fast, 15-minute tactical synchronization meeting. In modern distributed organizations, however, it frequently breaks down:

- **Drawn-Out, Exhausting Calls:** Detailed technical discussions between two engineers hijack everyone's focus, turning what should be a 15-minute check-in into a 40-minute drag.
- **Hybrid & Remote Friction:** Engineers operating in different time zones or on flexible schedules struggle with rigid morning calendar holds without losing track of peers' deliverables.
- **Buried Blockers:** Impediments are often whispered meekly at the end of an individual update and forgotten immediately once the call ends, stalling sprint milestones.
- **Zero Persistent History:** Verbal updates leave no written record, forcing project leads to track down status updates manually in chat channels.

To make standup ceremonies crisp, transparent, and actionable, I developed **DailyYrd** ([GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Yared98/dailyyrd) and live at [daily.yared.com.br](https://daily.yared.com.br/)).

## Dual Operational Modes: Asynchronous + Live Synchronous

DailyYrd bridges the gap between async communication and high-energy live alignment:

### 1. Asynchronous Mode (1-Minute Morning Check-in)
At the start of the workday, engineers submit three core updates in under a minute:
- **What was accomplished yesterday?**
- **What is the primary focus for today?**
- **Are there any technical blockers or external dependencies?**
Supports rich Markdown syntax, mood indicators (*Full Focus, Motivated, Need Coffee, etc.*), and instant autosaving.

### 2. Blocker Spotlight (Immediate Triage for Tech Leads)
Team members with blockers are automatically elevated to a dedicated high-priority zone at the top of the board with an animated **Rose Coral** pulse (`#F43F5E`). Engineering managers and Tech Leads can instantly spot who is blocked and coordinate pairing sessions without detaining the entire squad on the call.

### 3. Live Meeting Mode with 90-Second Speaker Roulette
For synchronous morning syncs, the facilitator activates Live Meeting mode:
- **Randomized Roulette:** Shuffles speaking order with a single click, eliminating the predictable monotony of alphabetical roll calls.
- **90-Second Countdown Timer:** Enforces concise updates with gentle auditory and visual alerts as time elapses.
- **Real-Time Presence:** Synchronized indicator across all connected client browsers showing who is speaking and who is up next.
- **Confetti Celebration:** Concludes the meeting with celebratory confetti upon the final participant's completion.

## Architecture & Technical Specifications

Built with the same high-reliability Rust foundation as the rest of the **Yrd Agile Toolkit**:

| Layer | Technology | Architectural Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Backend** | **Rust (Axum + Tokio)** | Asynchronous event processing with broadcast channels for live presence and synchronized timers. |
| **Database** | **SQLite (rusqlite + WAL)** | High-concurrency, zero-configuration local database storing boards, check-ins, and daily history. |
| **Frontend** | **React + Vite + TypeScript** | Sleek UI styled with *Agile Cadence*, dark/light mode support, and native Portuguese and English translations. |
| **Export Engine** | **Markdown & Slack Block Kit Parser** | One-click export converting daily updates into rich, formatted text for Slack and Discord. |
